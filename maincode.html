Explore Destinations

Find your next adventure in Batangas.

Church • Taal

Minor Basilica and Parish of St. Martin of Tours

The largest Catholic church in the Philippines and Asia, standing at the heart of Taal Heritage Town.

Church • Santo Tomas

Parish and International Shrine of St. Padre Pio

A peaceful pilgrimage shrine known for its open-air native Filipino architecture and prayer spaces.

Church • Batangas City

Montemaria International Pilgrimage & Conference Center

Home to the towering Mother of All Asia statue overlooking Batangas Bay, with chapels and viewing areas.

Beach • San Juan

Laiya, San Juan

A popular coastal getaway famous for its long stretch of cream-white sand, clear waters, and beachfront resorts.

Beach • Lian

Matabungkay Beach, Lian

A classic Batangas beach destination well known for its shallow shoreline, floating bamboo rafts, and laid-back atmosphere.

Beach • Nasugbu

Fortune Island, Nasugbu

An island escape featuring turquoise waters, limestone cliffs, and iconic Grecian-style pillars overlooking the sea.

Nature • Taal & Talisay

Taal Volcano and Lake

One of the world's smallest active volcanoes, dramatically surrounded by the scenic waters of Taal Lake.

Nature • Mabini

Anilao, Mabini

Recognized as the birthplace of Philippine scuba diving, rich in vibrant coral reefs and marine biodiversity.

Nature • Nasugbu

Mount Batulao

A scenic mountain trail featuring rolling grassland ridges, cool breeze, and panoramic views of Batangas.
